**LSE Department of Methodology** \
**Winter Term 2027**

A 10-week course on using generative AI---large language models in particular---as a tool for social science research: core concepts, prompting, programmatic workflows, evaluation, and responsible academic use.

_The following information is subject to change. Check back for more information._
